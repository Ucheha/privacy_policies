CartWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

CartWise ("we", "our", "the app") is a shared shopping list application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

1. Data We Collect

When you use CartWise, we collect the following:

  • Account information — your email address and display name, used for authentication and to identify you to collaborators.
  • Shopping list data — lists, items, categories, and completion history that you create within the app.
  • Usage data — app interactions such as list completions and spending totals, used to provide statistics and smart suggestions.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the app's core features (lists, sharing, sync).
  • To enable real-time collaboration between shared list members.
  • To generate personal shopping statistics and smart suggestions.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Your shopping lists are synced across your devices and shared only with collaborators you explicitly invite.

4. Third-Party Services

CartWise uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and data storage. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements in the free version. May collect advertising identifiers and device information. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — manages premium subscriptions. Processes purchase data through Google Play. Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. Your shopping list data is only visible to you and the collaborators you invite. Third-party services listed above may collect data as described in their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be removed from our servers. Shared lists you contributed to may retain your display name on previously checked items.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

CartWise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
